Peutz

A private, single-user personal assistant

Peutz is a privately operated personal assistant. It runs on hardware owned by its operator and connects to that operator's own Google Account (Google Calendar and Google Tasks) to produce daily briefings and manage personal tasks.

It is not a public service, has no sign-up, and is not offered to or used by any third party.
