Privacy Policy

Peutz — personal assistant. Last updated: 27 September 2026.

Peutz is a private, single-user personal assistant operated by Joshua Fernandes ("the operator"). This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What it accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent, Peutz uses Google OAuth to access that owner's own Google Calendar and Google Tasks (read and write). It does not access, request, or store any other person's Google data.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to provide the operator with a personal daily briefing and to create, update, or complete the operator's own calendar events and tasks. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or product analytics.

Storage and security

OAuth refresh tokens and a local working copy of calendar and task data are stored on hardware owned and controlled by the operator. Access is limited to that machine.

Sharing and disclosure

No Google user data is shared, sold, rented, or transferred to any third party. It is not used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any machine-learning or AI model, and it is not processed by humans other than the operator.

Limited Use disclosure

Peutz's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The operator may revoke Peutz's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. All locally stored tokens and data are then deleted.
